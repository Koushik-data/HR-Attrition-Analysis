👥 Employee Attrition Analysis



1\. Overall Employee Attrition

The organization recorded an overall attrition rate of 16.1%, with 237 employees leaving out of 1470 total employees, indicating moderate workforce turnover.



2\. Department-Wise Attrition

The Sales department experienced the highest attrition with approximately 38.82% of total employee exits, followed by the Human Resources department. This suggests higher workload pressure and reduced employee satisfaction in customer-facing roles.



3\. Overtime Impact

Employees working overtime contributed to nearly 40.43% of total attrition cases, showing a strong relationship between excessive workload and employee turnover.



4\. Age Group Trends

Employees aged 25–35 years represented the highest attrition segment, contributing to nearly 45% of total exits. This indicates higher career mobility among younger professionals.



5\. Job Satisfaction Analysis

Employees with low job satisfaction ratings (1 \& 2) showed significantly higher attrition rates compared to employees with higher satisfaction scores, highlighting employee engagement as a critical retention factor.



6\. Salary \& Attrition

Employees in the mid-level salary range demonstrated higher turnover rates compared to high-income employees, suggesting compensation growth opportunities may influence retention.



7\. Gender Distribution

Attrition was relatively balanced across genders, with male employees accounting for approximately 60% of employee exits, mainly due to higher workforce representation.



8\. Work-Life Balance Impact

Employees reporting poor work-life balance contributed to over 60% of total attrition cases, indicating the importance of flexible work policies and employee wellness initiatives.



9\. Education Field Insights

Employees from Life Sciences and Medical backgrounds showed the highest attrition levels among all education fields, contributing significantly to workforce turnover.



10\. Business Impact

High employee attrition can increase recruitment costs, reduce operational efficiency, and impact overall productivity. The analysis highlights the need for proactive HR retention strategies and employee engagement programs.
